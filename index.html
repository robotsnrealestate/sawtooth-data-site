How a project runs

  1. You send the spec

    Task, place, hours, device, format. A sentence starts it. You get a plan and a quote back.

  2. We run a pilot

    A small batch first, so you check it against your own rules before anything scales.

  3. We collect

    People on the ground capture it. The annotation team labels it.

  4. You get the data

    In the format your pipeline reads, or as a store report your team can act on.
